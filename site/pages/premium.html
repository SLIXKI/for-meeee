How to get it

  3. Paste the key into Asheo → Premium → Activate License.

Before you buy

A key binds to one device and is not transferable, so treat it like a password. Devices left idle for three days release their seat automatically, which means a reinstall won't lock you out. If the licence server is unreachable, Asheo falls back to its built-in defaults rather than blocking you — it never holds your tool hostage to a network failure.
